## What to check before paying

1. Is this a new Rap performance or visuals for a song you already have?
2. Which exact model, native resolution, seconds, format and audio settings does the quote cover?
3. Is the displayed dollar figure a one-time payment, a weekly renewal or an annual-plan monthly equivalent?
4. What happens to failed/unknown tasks, and when do downloads expire?
5. Do you have rights to the photos, lyrics, recording and reference materials?

Platform credits are not comparable currencies. A 12-second Mini example and a 15-second Standard video are different jobs. An advertised template does not by itself establish music rights. We have not generated paid competitor samples for this list.
